Payment and Shipping
====================

Offer your customers suitable payment methods and shipping options in your OXID eShop. These functions are key components of the checkout process and have a significant impact on purchasing decisions.

In the “Payment and Shipping” section, you configure available payment methods and define shipping cost rules.

Practical examples show how to map different requirements in the checkout process – for example, country-specific shipping costs, surcharges for payment types, or combinations of weight and cart value.
